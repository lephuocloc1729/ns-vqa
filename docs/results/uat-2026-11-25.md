# UAT results (2026-11-25)

The UAT for checklist row 11. Figures come from `eval/uat/score_sus.py`, run on the form export and the moderator's
log. Aggregates only: no names, no IDs next to quotes, no photos or question text. The raw files stay in
`eval/runs/uat/` (gitignored) and are deleted after the defense.

## Setup

- **When and where:** 15 sessions on the evening of 2026-11-25, from about 17:50 to 00:25 Vietnam time (UTC+7).
  Each was a moderated video call with screen sharing, and the owner filled in the observation log. Task success
  is therefore **observed**, not self-reported.
- **Participants:** n = 15.
  - 12 students and 3 drivers. **No tester who knows traffic law** took part (see "Deviations from the plan").
  - Driving licence: 9 hold A1/A (motorbike), 6 hold B or higher (car).
  - Age: 12 are 18–24, 2 are 25–34 and 1 is 50 or older.
  - 12 use chat assistants daily, 2 sometimes and 1 never.
  - Devices: 8 on a computer, 7 on a phone.
- **System:** a temporary copy of the deployed system, run by the owner for the sessions and deleted afterwards so
  that the sessions would not affect signcite.xyz.
  - It ran the same API and web images as signcite.xyz, built from `main` after PR #52 ("xe máy" means a
    two-wheeler). No later merge before the sessions changed `vqa/`, `app/`, `kb/`, `prompts/`, `web/` or
    `docker/`.
  - The pipeline is the frozen phase52 one. It had 10 answer slots and follow-up questions on.
  - The fallback was the RAG on a rented GPU (RTX 5090, Qwen3.5-9B Q4_K_M, 1 slot).
- **Tasks:**
  1. 5 fixed tasks, written as situations, on 3 of the owner's street photos (`eval/uat/task_sheet.md`), handed
     out one at a time.
  2. 2 free questions on one photo: one of the owner's spare photos (13 testers) or the tester's own photo (2).
  3. The form (`eval/uat/form_questions.md`): SUS, CSAT, and each task's completion and ease.
- **Expected answers on the fixed photos:** recorded in the dry runs on signcite.xyz (`eval/uat/moderator_notes.md`).
  Every fixed task was answered by the rules:
  - T1: P.131a;
  - T2 and T3: P.103a, no for a car and yes for a motorbike;
  - T4: P.127, 50 km/h.

## What went well

- **The legal basis was found by everyone.** T2 needs the verdict and the law and article, which are folded under
  the answer. 15 of 15 found them, with ease 4.8.
- **The follow-up and the sign boxes worked.** T3 asks a short follow-up and points at the deciding sign on the
  photo. 15 of 15 managed it, with ease 4.9. Since PR #52 the rules answer it directly ("Được. Biển P.103a … không
  áp dụng cho xe máy."), instead of handing it to the RAG.
- **Phones were nearly as easy as computers:** SUS 89.6 against 92.8, and task success 94% against 100%.

## Deviations from the plan

- **No tester who knows traffic law.** The plan asked for 2–3. The owner could not recruit any in time. The legal
  correctness of the answers was therefore not judged by an expert in this UAT. That evidence comes from the private
  benchmark, which the owner graded by hand.
- **The sessions ran on a temporary copy of the system,** not on signcite.xyz, and that copy was deleted afterwards.
  The tester accounts on signcite.xyz (`p01`–`p15`) were not used for the sessions. The log and the form can't be
  matched to the questions actually asked, and the server-side figures above are missing.
- **Everything ran in one evening** (2026-11-25), not over 26–28/11.

## Threats to validity

- **Small sample (n = 15):** the CIs are wide, and nothing is claimed beyond this group.
- **A young, AI-familiar group:** 12 of 15 are 18–24 and 12 of 15 use chat assistants daily. The testers were
  recruited from the team's contacts and are not a random sample of drivers. A high SUS from such a group is
  expected, and it may overstate ease for older or less technical users.
- **No qualitative feedback:** the open questions were blank, and the log has no tags or notes. The scores say the
  app was easy to use, but not why, and not what to improve.
- **No server records:** the wait times and the rules/RAG split during the sessions can't be checked. The times in
  the task table are the moderator's stopwatch.
- **The SUS wording is our Vietnamese translation,** not a validated one. Each item keeps the original's direction.
- **The fixed photos' answers were checked in advance** (dry runs). The fixed tasks test the interface, not the
  model.
